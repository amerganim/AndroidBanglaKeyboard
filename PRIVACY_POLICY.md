# Privacy Policy — Amader Bangla Keyboard

_Last updated: 2026-06-06_

Amader Bangla Keyboard ("the app") is an on-screen keyboard (input method) for
Android. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

## Summary

**The app does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data. Everything stays
on your device.** The app has **no internet permission**, so it is technically
incapable of sending your typing anywhere.

## What the app processes

To function as a keyboard, the app processes the text you type **only on your
device**, in memory, to:

- transliterate romanized input into Bangla;
- show word suggestions and next-word predictions;
- learn the words and word-pairs you use, to improve suggestions over time.

## What is stored on your device

The following is stored **locally** in the app's private storage and never leaves
your device:

- words you have typed (your personal dictionary);
- counts of how often you pick suggestions (learning data);
- next-word ("bigram") statistics learned from your typing;
- your key-size preference.

You can remove a learned word at any time by **long-pressing it** in the
suggestion bar. Uninstalling the app deletes all of this data.

## Sensitive fields

In password fields and fields that opt out of personalized learning, the app does
**not** show suggestions and does **not** learn or save anything.

## Permissions

- **Bind input method** (`BIND_INPUT_METHOD`): required for any keyboard; granted
  by the system when you enable the keyboard.
- **Microphone** (`RECORD_AUDIO`): requested **only** when you tap the voice-input
  (🎤) key, for voice typing. If you never use voice typing, it is never requested.
- The app requests **no internet permission** and **no contacts/location/storage**
  permissions.

## Voice typing (speech-to-text)

Voice typing is **optional**. When you tap the 🎤 key, the app uses **your device's
system speech-recognition service** (for example, the Google speech service that
ships with your phone) to convert speech to text. That recognized text is inserted
into the field you are typing in.

The app itself does not record, store, or transmit audio. However, the **system
speech service** may process your audio according to **its own** privacy policy,
and depending on your device it may send audio to its provider's servers to perform
recognition. If you prefer your voice never to leave the device, simply don't use
the 🎤 key — all other features work fully offline.

## Data sharing

None. No analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs that collect data.

## Children's privacy

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
